LO: Saltlord Edition
Android adaptation, foldable/touch UX and branding integration: Saltlord. AI-assisted development.

This edition includes native ARM64 gameplay; adjustable 30/60 fps; cover-screen ultrawide and inner-screen 4:3; separate editable touch layouts; invisible floating sticks; haptics; custom driver ZIP import; shader/pipeline caches; and on-demand reports. Driver downloads and guided all-disc/DLC setup are not included yet.
